The Short Version

We collect nothing.

Mood Orb does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data — ever. Your breath is entirely your own.

This is not a simplification. There is no fine print. Mood Orb was built from the ground up with a single privacy principle: the app exists entirely on your device and nowhere else.

No data collected No analytics No tracking No third-party SDKs Works fully offline

Third Parties

Who we share data with

No one. Mood Orb contains zero third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, advertising frameworks, or data brokers of any kind. There is nothing to share because nothing is collected.

The app makes no network requests of any kind during normal operation.

Your Device

What stays on your iPhone

Mood Orb stores one single piece of information locally on your device: whether the intro animation has been shown. This is a simple true/false value stored in your device's local preferences (UserDefaults). It never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

That is the complete and total extent of Mood Orb's data footprint.

Children

Users of all ages

Mood Orb is rated 4+ and is safe for users of all ages. Because we collect no data whatsoever, there are no special considerations required for younger users. The app is equally private for everyone.

Changes

Updates to this policy

If this privacy policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Given the nature of Mood Orb, meaningful changes to this policy are unlikely — there is very little to say about data we do not collect.
